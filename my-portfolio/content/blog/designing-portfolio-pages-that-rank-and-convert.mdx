A portfolio site has two audiences: the humans deciding whether to hire you,
and the crawlers/answer engines deciding whether to surface you at all. Most
portfolios optimize for the first and ignore the second entirely.

## Structure before style

Before touching layout, get the information architecture right:

- One clear H1 per page that states what you do
- Semantic sections (`about`, `work`, `writing`, `contact`) instead of an
  undifferentiated scroll
- Descriptive, keyword-relevant page titles and meta descriptions

## Make the content machine-readable

Structured data (JSON-LD for `Person`, `WebSite`, and `BlogPosting`) helps
both traditional search and AI answer engines understand who you are and
what you've built — not just that a page exists.

## Then design

Once the structure holds up, motion and visual polish are additive, not load
bearing. Ship the skeleton first.
