Motion should earn its place on the page. Every animation is a cost —
CPU/GPU cycles, layout thrash risk, and a delay between "content exists" and
"content is usable."

## A simple budget

- Prefer CSS transforms/opacity over properties that trigger layout
- Keep entrance animations under ~600ms
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Where it actually helps

Motion is most valuable as feedback (a button confirming a click) or as
orientation (a section fading in as it enters view) — not as decoration.
